Our Story

Made with Integrity,
Every Single Batch

100 Organics was built on one conviction — Indians deserve food that is genuinely what it claims to be. We grow our own sugarcane and moringa, carefully source other organic ingredients, and manufacture everything ourselves so nothing is left to chance.

  • Sugarcane and moringa grown on our own farm — no pesticides, no chemicals
  • All other ingredients sourced exclusively from verified organic suppliers
  • Every incoming ingredient tested for purity before entering our facility
  • In-house manufacturing only — we never outsource production
  • No artificial colours, flavours, preservatives, or fillers — ever
  • Third-party lab testing on every batch before it ships
Our Farm

What We Grow Ourselves

Two key crops at the heart of our products come directly from our own land — grown the traditional, chemical-free way.

🌾

Sugarcane

Our sugarcane is grown without synthetic fertilisers or pesticides. It goes directly into our processing unit where it is cold-crushed and slow-boiled to produce pure, mineral-rich jaggery — preserving everything nature put in.

🚜 Own Farm Grown
🌿

Moringa

Often called the "miracle tree", our moringa is cultivated naturally on our farm. Leaves are carefully harvested, shade-dried, and milled in-house into a nutrient-dense powder — retaining its full protein, iron, and antioxidant profile.

🚜 Own Farm Grown
🤝

Everything Else — Responsibly Sourced

For ingredients we don't grow ourselves — millets, sesame, dry fruits, spices — we work exclusively with trusted organic farmers and certified suppliers. Every incoming batch is verified for organic origin and tested for purity before entering our manufacturing facility. Honest sourcing is as important to us as honest manufacturing.
